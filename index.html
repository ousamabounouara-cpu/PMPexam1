About the exam

  • 180 scenario-based questions covering People, Process and Business Environment, including case-study questions and multiple-select questions.
  • Exam time: 4 hours (240 minutes). The countdown is always visible at the top of the screen. When it reaches zero the exam is submitted automatically.
  • One break: 20 minutes. Press Take break at any point (you will also be reminded at question 90). The exam clock is paused during the break, and the break ends automatically after 20 minutes or when you press Resume exam.
  • Use NEXT → to go to the next question and ← Previous to go back. You can flag a question for review and jump to any question from the navigator.
  • Multiple-select questions say how many answers to choose; all correct answers are required to score the question.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser, so a page refresh will not lose your answers or time.
  • Results and the correct answers are shown only after you finish.

Tips: Pace yourself at roughly 80 seconds per question, answer every question (there is no penalty for guessing), and flag the hard ones to revisit.
